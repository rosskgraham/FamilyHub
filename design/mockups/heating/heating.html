B · The header's other states (story 1)

Mode and Eco are only shown, never changed. With sample data, the Hub shows the first state. If the Nest hasn't reported for 15 minutes, the header says so instead of showing an old temperature.

C · The Heating panel (story 2)

Opened by tapping the heating in the header. − and + move the target 0.5° at a time, between 16° and 22°. The Hub sends the new target once the taps stop, like turning the dial, and it lasts until the Nest's next scheduled change. Mode, Eco and the schedule stay in the Nest app.
