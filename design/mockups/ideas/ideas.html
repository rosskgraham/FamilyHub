A · The Ideas tab (Day theme)

Ideas comes after Games in the bottom bar. On the left: send an idea or say something's broken. On the right: every idea, with who sent it, where it is in kid words over five steps, and Me too (AB#117), with the most backed first, then the newest. Me too stops once an idea is being built, and the count stays. "Not this time" is faded and goes after a week; "It's on the Hub!" goes after two. Something not sent yet says so.

B · Something's broken

Tapping "Something's broken" opens the form with the keyboard. Tap who you are and which part, then say what happened, 200 characters at most. Send is greyed until all three are done. Who's chosen first shows how many they have left today.

E · Me too

Tapping Me too asks who's backing it. The person who sent it can't back their own. Anyone who already has shows a tick, and tapping them again takes it back. The count on the card on the board changes with it.
