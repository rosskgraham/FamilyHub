A · Sleep and Lights out in Settings (Day theme)

Below Theme, above Household and Google. The defaults: Sleep 22:00–06:30, Lights out 00:00–06:00. Each − and + moves a time by 30 minutes and saves straight away, like the Clock and Theme choices. Times follow the clock format chosen above.

C · Lights out stays inside Sleep

Lights out can't start before Sleep or end after it. At the edge its button is greyed and a line says why, like the heating's 22° limit. Here Lights out runs to 06:30, where Sleep ends. Moving Sleep's times in past Lights out moves Lights out with them.
