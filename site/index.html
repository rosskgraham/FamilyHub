FamilyHub

A screen on one family's kitchen wall that shows the day at a glance, so nobody has to reach for a phone.

What it does

  • Shows everyone's day and week from the family's Google Calendars.
  • Keeps the Shopping list and the family's To-dos, from Google Tasks, on the wall, to tick off there or on a phone.
  • Tracks the Chores, the weather, and the house's energy use.

Who it's for

FamilyHub is a personal project for one household. It isn't a product or a service, and nobody outside the household signs in to it. It runs on a Raspberry Pi in the house, and nothing on it can be reached from the internet.

It signs in to the household's own Google account to read its Calendars and Tasks. Read the privacy policy for exactly what it reads and where that goes.

The code

All of it is open source, on GitHub: github.com/rosskgraham/FamilyHub.
