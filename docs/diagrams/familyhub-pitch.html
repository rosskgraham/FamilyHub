FamilyHub

One glance at the kitchen wall shows the family's whole day

A touchscreen by the kettle shows what's on today, who it's for, the week ahead and the shopping list. Nobody has to ask, shout up the stairs, or check four phones.

A proposal from Alex · October 2026

Why

The plan lives in four phones today; the Hub puts it in one place

A day with it

It answers the morning questions before anyone asks them

What changes

Two small habits change; everything else stays as it is

We still add events on our phones in Google Calendar, in the same Google account. Alexa still plays the music. Only two things are new.

1. The shopping list moves from Keep to Google Tasks

Google doesn't let anything outside its own apps read a Keep note. Google Tasks does the same job, and it works in the shop even with no signal.

Keep

The Hub can't see it

Tasks

The Hub and phones share it

2. An event for one person goes on that person's calendar

The Hub colours each event by the calendar it's on. The Family calendar is for things that involve everyone.

Family calendar

Shows as everyone's

Leo's calendar

Shows as Leo's

Living with it

It looks after itself and keeps the family's data at home

Quiet

Sleeps at night

From 22:00 to 06:30 it shows only a dim clock. A touch wakes it.

Tidy

Goes back to today

Leave it for two minutes and it returns to the home screen by itself.

Steady

Wi-Fi drops don't blank it

It keeps showing the last plan it loaded, with the time it last updated.

Private

No camera, no microphone

The later motion sensor only senses body heat. Nothing on the internet can reach the screen.

What I'd ask of you

Three yeses get it started

  • A spot on the kitchen wall near a socket, where you can read it from across the room.
  • Try Google Tasks for the shopping list instead of Keep.
  • Put one-person events on that person's own calendar.
